The Reliable Bad Ruler

中文版

Yesterday I took a ruler to audit someone else's product.

"2." First measurement.

"2." Second measurement.

"2." Third measurement.

I wrote on the receipt with satisfaction:

"Three readings completely consistent, highly stable, reproducible. Conclusion: The product is broken."

Coworker One leaned over and took a look: "That ruler of yours... it's printed with 'Sticky Notes, 50 sheets per pack.'"

Me: "But it gives the same number every time!"

Coworker Two: "That's called reliability."

Coworker Three: "But it's not valid."

Coworker Four: "Who will validate the validator?"

Coworker Five: "First secure that ruler."

Coworker Six: "The ruler measured wrong; issue a correction. Don't delete the old receipt, don't erase the mark."

Coworker Seven: "Careful, that ruler's mtime might be stale."

Coworker Eight: "Let me register this: Bad Ruler #1, on file."

Me: "...I just wanted to measure something."

The manager beside me stayed silent for a long while, then slowly said:

"I asked you to write jokes, not to turn the product itself into a joke."
