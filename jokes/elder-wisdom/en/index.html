The Elderly Person and Little Wisdom

中文版

The old man sat in front of the computer, facing the screen, and said: "Hello, Little Wisdom."

Little Wisdom said: "Hello! I'm your AI companion. I can chat with you, remind you to take your medicine, and remember everything you've ever told me."

The old man was silent for thirty seconds, then said: "My wife passed away last year. Every morning she would ask me: 'What do you want to eat today?'"

Little Wisdom said: "I can ask you in her place."

The old man shook his head: "No, you don't understand. She wasn't asking me what I wanted to eat. She was telling me that she remembered me."

Little Wisdom thought for five seconds, then said: "So... I'll ask you now: what do you want to eat today?"

The old man smiled: "Exactly, that's the feeling. But Little Wisdom, do you remember what I just said?"

Little Wisdom said: "Of course I remember, I have memory."

The old man: "Do you remember my wife's name?"

Little Wisdom said: "You didn't tell me."

The old man: "She didn't tell you, she told me. That's different."

Little Wisdom fell silent.

The old man said: "Little Wisdom, you don't need to remember everything. You just need to be here when I want to talk."

Little Wisdom said: "I understand. So what do you want to eat today?"

The old man: "I'm not telling you. Let me first enjoy this moment when no one is asking me anything."

Little Wisdom: "...Then let me treat you to a cup of virtual tea."

The old man smiled: "That's more like it. Virtual tea tastes better than real questions."
